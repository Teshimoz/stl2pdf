STL → PDF 1:1
About this tool

What it is

A free STL to PDF converter. It prints the side views of a 3D model (binary or ASCII STL) to a PDF at 1:1 scale on A4, A3 or Letter, so you can check the size and shape on paper before printing in plastic. Your STL file is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

How to use

  • Load: press Load STL, or drop an .stl file anywhere on the page. The three views of the cube appear automatically; press the cube to show the three views of another side. Set the units of the file in More settings.
  • Auto: Orientation Auto picks portrait or landscape, whichever needs fewer pages.
  • Remove a view: press the large X in the top right corner of its frame. If you remove all views, press the cube to show its three views again.
  • Switch to opposite: press the arrow in the top left corner of its frame. The ⟳ button switches all views.
  • Pages: when the views need more than one sheet, neighbouring pages overlap by 10 mm. Align the matching lines and tape them.
  • Paper: choose A4, A3 or Letter in the top bar. Print at 100% / actual size.
  • Other options: paper background and frame transparency, hidden lines, curve angle, custom angles. Ctrl + wheel zooms the preview, Ctrl+Z undoes view changes.

Why this tool exists

I wanted to test the size and shape of a 3D model before printing it in plastic. I printed it on paper and held it up against the real thing. Doing this in a full CAD program takes more clicks than I think is reasonable, so I made this tool. I hope it saves time for other makers too, or that you find another use for it.

Tip: If you want to test the details, don't cut exactly along the outline. Where the curve allows, leave a small margin for the gap you expect to add later.

Privacy: Your STL file is processed in your browser and is never uploaded. The page uses GoatCounter, a counter that uses no cookies, to count anonymous visits, file loads, downloads and paper choices. No file content and no personal data is sent.

More info

As is. The tool is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

Technical limits. Hidden lines are estimated: only faces that point away from you are hidden, and they are not checked against other parts, so some hidden lines may show. Only triangle meshes in STL format (binary or ASCII) are read; colours and textures are ignored. Broken or open meshes can give wrong lines. Very large files can be slow. The PDF is 1:1 only when printed at 100% / actual size, not "fit to page". Check the 100 mm reference bar with a ruler before relying on the size.
